Motor HA – integration

Each window with a shade is a device in Home Assistant. A wizard with 7 circles and pictures, changes with Configure, the card added to a dashboard by itself, the Motor HA card bundled.

Works with every shade motor in Home Assistant (cover.*): Zigbee, Z-Wave, Tuya, Shelly, Somfy and more. Windows with one or two sashes.

Calibration and card on the dashboard

The motor speed is picked from the shade device (e.g. Moes). Want it exact? On the window device page press Calibrate (point 2). The shade goes up, down and up, and the measured time is saved by itself. 🤖 Calibrate now in the wizard does the same.

On the same page, Add to dashboard (point 3) puts the window card on a dashboard (Home Assistant 2026.6 or newer), and ⚙️ Visit (point 1) opens the circles wizard.

How to control

IconWhat you doWhat happens
Drag the handle at the bottom of one sashOnly that sash moves
Drag the round handle in the middleBoth sashes move
Swipe on the glassBoth sashes move
Click STOP or touch the window while movingEverything stops
Click the up arrowGoes all the way up: window uncovered
Click the down arrowGoes all the way down: window covered
Click ▲ 5% or ▼ 5%Moves a little up or down
Click a preset, e.g. 75%Goes to that position

You can also open and close the window like any shade: on the device page, by voice or in an automation. Both sashes move together.

Glossary: what do these words mean?

WordIn plain words
Home Assistant (HA)The program that runs your home.
HACSThe add-on “store” for HA. You download the integration from here.
IntegrationAn add-on that adds new devices to HA. Here: windows.
DeviceOne window, e.g. “Okno Living room”. See Settings → Devices & services.
EntityOne thing of a device that HA sees and controls. A window has a shade entity and a Calibrate button.
Add deviceThe button on the integration page: adds another window.
ConfigureThe button next to a window: changes its settings.
VisitThe link on the window device page: opens the circles wizard.
CardA tile on the HA screen. Here: a realistic window with a shade.
DashboardThe HA screen where cards live.
Window sensorA sensor on the window: knows if it is closed, tilted or open.
Rolled up / 100%Shade all the way up, window uncovered.
Lowered / 0%Shade all the way down, window covered.

Questions and problems

“Motor HA” is not in the integration list

After downloading from HACS you must restart HA (step 4). On a phone, close and reopen the app.

What to click:
  1. The ⏻ icon at the top right
  2. Restart

Settings → System. Wait 1–2 minutes.

The card is not in the “Add card” list

The card loads by itself with the integration, nothing to refresh. Check that HA was restarted after installing (step 4). Then pencil, Add card and type Okno.

What to click:
  1. Pencil: edit the dashboard
  2. Type Okno
  3. Click the Motor HA card

Then pick the window, e.g. Okno Living room.

The animation is faster or slower than the shade

On the window device page press Calibrate. The shade goes up, down and up, and the time is saved by itself.

What to click:
  1. Visit: the 7-circle wizard
  2. Calibrate: measure travel time
  3. Add to dashboard: card on a dashboard

Calibration: the shade goes up, down and up. The time is saved by itself.

No weather behind the glass

Configure → step 4: pick your weather or leave it empty. Empty = internet weather for your home.
What to click:
  1. Weather outside
  2. Next

Empty = internet weather for your home.

The window sensor does not work

Configure → step 2: pick the sensor and how the window opens: tilted or opened on hinges.
What to click:
  1. Number of sashes
  2. Window sensors and how the window opens
  3. Next

No sensors? Leave empty.

The sashes are swapped (left is right)

Configure → step 1: remove both shades and pick them again: left first, then right.
What to click:
  1. Enter the window name
  2. Pick the shade (or two: left, right)
  3. Next

Single-sash window: pick one shade.

The shade jumps to 0% or 100% while moving

These are bad motor reports. The card ignores them. If it still jumps, report a bug and name the motor.

The card is in Polish but I want English

The card uses the language of your HA profile. You can also open Advanced in the card editor and pick English in Card language.

What to click:
  1. Open Advanced
  2. Card language: pick a language

auto = the language of your HA profile.

No answer here? Report a bug or write on the HA forum.

Part 2. For advanced users

You do not need this. The wizard has set everything. Details below.

What the integration creates

WhatDescription
Device “Okno …”One per window (each entry). Manufacturer: Motor HA, model: shade type.
Entity cover.okno_…The window shade. Position = average of the sashes. Open, close, stop and position go to both sash shades at once.
Button CalibrateMeasures the real travel time of the shade and saves it.
Attribute okno_haWindow settings for the card. The card reads them by itself.
Device “Motor HA”One for the whole integration, with one 2-in-1 update (integration and card together). When installed from HACS, HACS shows the update.
Circles wizardA 7-circle page: the “Visit” link on the window device or Configure. No sidebar item.
Motor HA cardBundled. Loads by itself on every dashboard, no resource needed.

The window in automations

The window is a normal cover in HA. In an automation pick Cover: set position (or open / close) and the window entity, e.g. Okno Living room. Both sash shades move together.

The bundled Motor HA card

The integration includes the Motor HA card. No separate install and no resource needed. Light and dark look, weather behind the glass, day and night.

Already have the Motor HA card from HACS? No problem. Both can be installed.

Installation without HACS (manual)

  1. Download the latest release (Source code, zip).
  2. Copy the custom_components/okno_ha folder into config/custom_components/ in HA (e.g. with the File editor or Samba add-on).
  3. Restart HA and click Add integration (installation step 5).

Removing

One window: Settings → Devices & services → Motor HA, next to the window ⋮ → Delete. The whole integration: delete all windows, then in HACS ⋮ → Remove and restart HA. Your real shades stay unchanged.
